## What players would know

Arnhold Vaelric, known as the **Warden of Thorns**, is a half-elf warlord who controls a Fey-adjacent gate route in the wilderness. He extracts tolls from travelers and caravans and is tolerated because removing him would cost more than ignoring him—until it doesn’t.

### Common rumors

- The toll is never just coin.
- People come back missing something they can’t name.
