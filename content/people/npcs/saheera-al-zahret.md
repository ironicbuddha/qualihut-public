## What players would know

Saheera al-Zahret is a prominent desert matriarch associated with [Traveler](../../factions/travelers.md) politics and caravan-route negotiations. In settled lands she is spoken of as a “caravan queen.” Among route people, she is spoken of as someone who can make an argument about water sound like law.
