## What players would know

Oskar Damm runs [The Broken Seal](../../locations/broken-seal-tavern.md) in [Niederstadt](../../locations/niederstadt.md). He looks like a man who never
gets surprised twice: plain clothes, plain speech, and eyes that do the counting
before the coins hit the table.
