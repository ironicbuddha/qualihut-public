## What players would know

### Portrait (player-safe)

![Ariette Marot](images/ariette-marot.png)

Ariette Marot is a Marcher-born broker of cloth, accent, and presentation who
has survived long enough in the capital to make provincial style look effortless
and respectable. She can source the right marsh colors, explain why a room is
reacting badly, and tell whether someone is really from the Marches before they
finish their first drink.

### Common rumors

- Ariette charges more to preserve a provincial touch than to remove one.
- She can tell who is really from the Marches by how they hold a cup and where
  they go quiet in a song.
