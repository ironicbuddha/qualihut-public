## What players would know

### Portrait (player-safe)

![Magister Lio Greven](images/lio-greven.png)

Magister Lio Greven is an itinerant arithmancer with a nervous scholar's hands,
a weather-stained robe, and the anxious habit of correcting counts no one asked
him to make.

He travels with a squat [Styglian Enumerator](../../monsters/styglian-enumerator.md)
named Nineteen, which he describes as "a bonded tally-witness" whenever someone
calls it a pet.

### Common rumors

- Greven can count a spilled sack of beans faster than most people can curse
  about it.
- Nineteen follows him because Greven owns the contract, not because Greven is
  in charge.
- Greven has never admitted to being lost. He has only been "temporarily
  misindexed."
