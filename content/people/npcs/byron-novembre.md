## What players would know

![Byron Novembre](images/byron-novembre.png)

Byron Novembre is the Chief of Station in
[Valdengratz](../../locations/valdengratz.md). He conducts business with quiet,
procedural efficiency, asking direct questions and expecting clear answers.
His manner is reserved and unsentimental; he offers little ceremony or
reassurance.

## Appearance

Byron Novembre is a half-elf in late middle age, standing five feet nine inches
tall, with a slight build and sharp features. His hairline recedes at the
temples, where his hair has turned grey.
