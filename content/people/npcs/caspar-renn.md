## What players would know

### Portrait (player-safe)

![Caspar Renn](images/caspar-renn.png)

Caspar Renn is an arranger and lecturer with a reputation for taking rough
regional material and making it performable in respectable rooms. He is one of
those capital men who can speak admiringly about frontier art for ten minutes
without ever sounding as if he would trust the people who made it.

### Common rumors

- If Caspar writes a tune down, it stops frightening the city.
- He can hear a song once and tell you which parts civilization will not permit.
