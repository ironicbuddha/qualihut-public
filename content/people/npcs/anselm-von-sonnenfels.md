## What players would know

Hochpriester Anselm von Sonnenfels, of
[House von Sonnenfels](../../institutions/house-von-sonnenfels.md), is the
[Solar Church](../../institutions/solar-church.md)’s high priest: a public
symbol of doctrine, continuity, and the promise that the Empire’s rituals still
mean what they used to mean.

He is the face people imagine when they think of official faith: immaculate robes, measured speech, and an authority that feels older than the argument you’re trying to have.
