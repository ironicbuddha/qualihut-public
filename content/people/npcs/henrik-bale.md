## What players would know

Henrik Bale looks more like a carpenter having a bad week than a mercenary,
right up until he starts examining hinges, chains, and wagon frames with
professional interest.

### Common rumors

- Henrik can look at a locked crate and tell you whether it was built to keep
  thieves out or buyers honest.
