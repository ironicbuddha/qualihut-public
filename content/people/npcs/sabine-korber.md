## What players would know

### Portrait (player-safe)

![Sabine Korber](images/sabine-korber.png)

Sabine Korber is a capital hostess known for elegant small-room evenings where
musicians, minor officials, ambitious young nobles, and carefully selected
provincials are made to seem as if they belong in the same moral weather. She
has a reputation for "discovering" rough talent and presenting it in a way the
city can admire without feeling threatened.

### Common rumors

- If Sabine praises your "natural force," she is about to remove whatever made
  you dangerous.
- An invitation from Sabine can mean opportunity, inspection, or both.
