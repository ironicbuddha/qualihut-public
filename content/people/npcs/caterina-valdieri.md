## What players would know

Caterina Valdieri is Giovanni Valdieri’s wife and the public face of “the family”
when Giovanni is being the public face of “the bank.” At court she reads as
composed, tasteful, and impossibly well-briefed: a woman who remembers names the
way a clerk remembers debts.

### Common rumors

- She can tell who you are by the way you sign your name.
- The Valdieri don’t have “servants”; they have witnesses.
