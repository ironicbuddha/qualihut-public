## What players would know

### Portrait (player-safe)

![Doc Benway](images/doc-benway.png)

Doc Benway is an undercity patch-doctor: the kind of man called when someone
needs stitching, splints, a lie for the Watch, or a second opinion no temple
would sign. He is competent, unsentimental, and widely believed to have saved
more lives than he has ruined, though no one agrees on the ratio.

### Common rumors

- Benway tells you the price before he tells you the chances.
- If Benway says someone is already dead, he usually means the paperwork has not
  caught up yet.
