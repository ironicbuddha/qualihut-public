## What players would know

Ada Serli manages [The Second Button](../../locations/the-second-button.md).
A broad human woman in her late fifties, she wears a plum waistcoat, silver
spectacles on a cord, and one conspicuously plain brass button among the pearl
buttons at her cuffs. Her gray hair is braided close to her head. She usually
carries a small cup of bitter coffee that has gone cold.

Ada greets regulars with easy affection and newcomers with attentive curiosity.
She enjoys a good gambling story, particularly one in which the teller lost.
When a dispute interrupts play, she brings over a chair and sits down to hear it.
The dealers wait for her decision.

“Welcome, love. Who should I thank for sending you?”

### Common rumors

- She once ran a pawnshop and can price a necklace without taking it off you.
- She has turned away guests rich enough to buy the whole street.
- Ada remembers the first thing you ever pawned to her. She may remember the
  reason you gave her, too.
