## What players would know

Ambasciatore Lorenzo di Monteluce is the [Southern Union](../../factions/southern-union.md)’s ambassador to the Empire: polished, cultured, and publicly deferential to imperial tradition. He smiles the way rich men smile in other people’s houses—warmly, as if nothing could possibly be at stake.
