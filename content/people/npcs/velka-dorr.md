## What players would know

Velka Dorr is a Brazen Pike scout and convoy rider who looks impatient even
when standing still.

She was the party's first point of contact on the Broadbarrel road and reads
the verge like other people read a ledger.

### Common rumors

- If someone breaks from the road and thinks distance will save them, Velka is
  usually the answer.
