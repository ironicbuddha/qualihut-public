## What players would know

Velka Dorr is a [Brazen Pike](../../factions/brazen-pike-company.md) scout and convoy rider who looks impatient even
when standing still.

She was the party's first point of contact on the [Broadbarrel](../../institutions/broadbarrel-caravan.md) road and reads
the verge like other people read a ledger.

### Common rumors

- If someone breaks from the road and thinks distance will save them, Velka is
  usually the answer.
