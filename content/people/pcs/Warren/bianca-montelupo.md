## What players would know

![Bianca Montelupo](Bianca.png)

Bianca Montelupo is a compact, unsentimental druid with a raven familiar and a
procedural attitude toward “nature magic”: boundaries, cycles, and the price of
interference.

She currently operates under imperial paperwork (Joint Task Force / continuity
authority), with the practical implication that she goes where she’s told and
asks the questions she’s permitted to ask.

### Common rumors

- She doesn’t talk about her home grove.
- She trusts procedures more than promises.
