## Session 4 Recap: Broadbarrel Road

The investigation left Hochsilvar behind and turned into an embedded road job.

## Read-Aloud Recap

You briefed your handler on what you had learned: the vault breach relied on
living Valdieri blood, Branka was tied to the road lane, and Broadbarrel was
the strongest live lead. The answer back was simple: you had done enough to
move. The city was now delay, not progress.

At that same briefing, Ishtar joined the team as an additional covert
operative.

With fresh horses and urgency on your side, you left Hochsilvar and pushed onto
the Broadbarrel route. On the road you picked up a strange omen: a mottled
rabbit that watched rather than fled, suggesting that the forest had noticed
you.

Instead of trying to hit the caravan from outside, you got ahead of it, waited,
and made contact under a plausible traveler cover story. That worked. You met
Velka Dorr first, then negotiated passage onto the
[Broadbarrel Caravan](../../institutions/broadbarrel-caravan.md) through
[Henrik Volmar](../../people/npcs/henrik-volmar.md).

By the end of the session, you were no longer chasing Broadbarrel from the
outside. You were inside it, watching how it moved, how its guards worked, and
how carefully Branka's people controlled the road.

## What players would know

- You reported your findings and got authorization to move immediately.
- [Ishtar](../../people/pcs/Niki/ishtar-sheet.md) joined the team.
- You caught up to Broadbarrel and successfully joined the caravan as paying
  travelers.
- You made first contact with [Velka Dorr](../../people/npcs/velka-dorr.md).
- [Henrik Volmar](../../people/npcs/henrik-volmar.md) admitted you onto the
  caravan.
- You confirmed that Branka's escort is disciplined, professional, and used to
  working together.
- You did **not** yet confirm exactly where the refined magic is hidden among
  the cargo.

## Current working picture

- Broadbarrel is still the correct lead.
- Branka is still central to the road side of the problem.
- The escort is too organized to treat as ordinary hired muscle.
- The best path forward is observation, patience, and finding the real load
  before forcing a confrontation.

## Active leads for next session

- Work out which cargo cluster actually matters.
- Watch whether Branka favors a particular wagon or section of the train.
- Learn the camp routine, wagon order, and weak points without getting read too
  early.
- Decide when embedded observation stops being enough and pressure has to
  escalate.
