## Session 11 Recap: White Stag and the Second Button

The party's cover had failed at customs. Byron Novembre offered a new direction:
operate as White Stag, a covert team the Empire could deny knowing. New identities
would be needed, and future work would depend more on the party's own contacts,
safe houses, and resources.

Silk and Terpsichore joined the group. Silk brought connections to merchants
and the underworld; Terpsichore brought careful observation, nature knowledge,
and practical healing. Silk recognised Esteban Caldera's name from smuggling
circles, though he did not know Caldera's affiliations.

Byron's priority was an alchemist named Matteo who wanted to defect from the
Southern Union. Matteo held a formula of particular interest to the Solar
Church and would only hand it over at the High Cathedral in Hochsilvar.
Byron described a long, rough journey through warlord-held country and said
Matteo was guarded and working against an unspecified deadline. White Stag
would be the name by which Matteo could recognise his rescuers.

A Fey gate might offer a way back. The party favoured saving that option for
the return, while keeping a road alternative in mind. Byron promised resources
for passage and gave them twenty-four hours to pursue the remaining stolen-magic
leads before setting out to meet the guide.

### Customs and the basilica

The group took back alleys to Seldon Finck's office. Fren asked her to hold the
caravan while headquarters checked the buried caches. Finck challenged the
case against Henrik and Branka: suspicious cargo did not, by itself, prove
which individual was responsible. Fren nevertheless disclosed the stolen
refined magic, despite Byron's concern about keeping its disappearance quiet.

Finck agreed there was enough circumstantial evidence to take further action.
The caravan's freight was already being split into different loads. She went
to stop it while the party turned to the other unfinished business: the
Bloodroot Grove.

At the Solar Basilica, Cardinal Hofstetter examined the red-veined quartz and
heard about the grove, the bell-like signal, and the disturbing dreams. He
identified necromantic influence but could not say exactly what it meant.
The Cult of the Red Sun was a possibility, not a confirmed explanation.
The bell might have marked the party or sent a message; he did not know which.

The cardinal recommended a full-day cleansing ritual and suggested that Nela
should also receive attention. The ritual was not performed. He provided a
sun amulet that could be raised with an incantation to offer brief protection
against undead or necromantic effects. Aurelia recorded the invocation.
The cardinal kept the quartz locked away, and Silk made a donation.

### Twenty-four recovered vials

Back at the station, Byron showed the reply from headquarters: the buried
caches had yielded **twenty-four vials of refined magic**, twelve per box.
The party's estimate of ten specially reinforced wagons suggested about
120 vials could have travelled that way—well short of the reported theft of
500. It still did not establish where the remaining magic had gone.

Byron issued three single-use ash letters, each linked to its own counterpart
at headquarters. Writing on a letter would transmit the words and consume it.
He also instructed a clerk to tell the city guard to detain Branka Voss.
No arrest was confirmed, and the party decided to look for her through its
own contacts rather than wait for the guard.

They sent Finck a local messenger with the cache confirmation and a request
to keep holding the relevant people or cargo where possible.

### An evening at the Second Button

Silk led the party underground, through a closing market to a high-end tailor's
shop. He vouched for his companions at its side entrance and brought them
through a concealed passage into the Second Button casino.

Silk played blackjack; Fren won at dice and celebrated with distilled flower
wine. Gashesh remained watchful, declining to drink even the water. The group
then entered the Mending Room, the casino's busier private salon. Silk greeted
its manager, Ada Serli, and recognised Oren Bell of the Third Key among the patrons.

Ada agreed to speak with them in a booth. They asked about Branka using the
story that she had betrayed fellow caravan travellers. Ada warned that Branka
was dangerous to know, then offered leads through Ponte Nero or La Casa dei
Tre Spilli. The party preferred the latter.

For a favour owed by Silk, Ada directed them to **the Warm Press**, a pressing
house near the downstream Stilt Shores. They should ask for **the third pin**.
The contact was **Nello Varr**, a fence Silk had met before who handled valuable
illicit merchandise. Ada agreed not to pass their enquiry to Oren.

## Where we left off

The party was still in the Second Button's Mending Room, around half past eight
to nine in the evening, preparing to follow the Warm Press lead.

- Branka had not been found or confirmed arrested.
- Headquarters had recovered twenty-four vials; the rest remained unaccounted for.
- The quartz was with Cardinal Hofstetter, and the cleansing ritual was still pending.
- The party had three unused ash letters and the sun amulet.
- Silk owed Ada a favour. Nello had not yet been contacted.
- The city investigation and preparations for the Union mission were still competing for time.
