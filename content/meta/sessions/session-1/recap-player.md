## Session 1 Recap: Hochsilvar

The team continued the [Banco Valdieri](../../../institutions/banco-valdieri.md) robbery investigation under heavy time
pressure during [the Royal Games](../../../institutions/royal-games.md).

## What happened

- You confirmed there was no useful surveillance record of the robbery itself.
- [Captain Ensio](../../../people/npcs/ensio-silbermark.md) shared that the most likely escape direction was toward
  [Niederstadt](../../../locations/niederstadt.md), likely using transport and smuggling channels.
- The party split to cover ground:
  - one track pursued social intel at the [Ivory Wheel](../../../locations/mano-fortunata-guildhouse.md) sportsbook;
  - one track followed the sewer route into Niederstadt.
- Sewer tracking and witness checks in the [Avenue of Butchers](../../../locations/niederstadt-avenue-of-butchers.md) confirmed a
  likely pair of couriers (man and woman) moving under heavy load earlier in
  the day.
- The sportsbook line did not produce a direct suspect, but helped establish
  event timing and crowd conditions around the Games.
- In the meeting with [Giovanni](../../../people/npcs/giovanni-valdieri.md), you confirmed that vault security can be
  satisfied by living Valdieri bloodline access, and that citywide lockdown was
  not feasible during active festival traffic.

## Current working theory

The robbery crew used planning, timing, and logistics to move cargo through
crowded city infrastructure rather than brute force alone.

  route.
